About me

Statistics, survey methods and applied research.

I am a statistician with an MSc and BSc in Statistics from Jagannath University. Since March 2025 I have worked as a Staff Researcher at the BRAC Institute of Educational Development (BRAC IED), where I contribute to data management, analysis and reporting for education studies.

Before BRAC IED, I worked at SURCH, a survey research house, on UNICEF-funded baseline and assessment surveys. I also supervised fieldwork and assisted with analysis of national survey data such as the Bangladesh Demographic and Health Surveys (BDHS).

I enjoy teaching data analysis, and I have trained learners in SPSS and R.
